# Bid Builder

The Bid Builder supports building bids on opportunities by connecting the Opportunity Tracker and Talent Wall.

## Features {#generate-bids}

- **Build bids based on opportunity and talent**: Assess and select candidates to put forward
- **Upload additional documentation**: Support bid building with background documents
- **Customise proposal sections**: Select and modify content sections
- **Modify content**: Use prompts or direct modifications
- **Export**: Download drafted bids to PDF or DOCX format

## Workflow

1. **Select an opportunity** from the Opportunity Tracker
2. **Review matched candidates** from the Talent Wall
3. **Select your team** for the bid
4. **Customise sections** as needed
5. **Generate the bid document**
6. **Export** to PDF or Word for final touches

## Collaboration Features

- Add notes against opportunities (candidates in consideration, reasons for no-bid)
- Add notes against talent (interaction notes, preferences)
- Track all changes and decisions

## Tips for Better Bids

1. **Keep talent profiles updated**: Better data = better candidate matching
2. **Add notes consistently**: Track decisions and rationale
3. **Use the AI summaries**: Quick understanding of opportunity requirements
4. **Review matched candidates**: The AI provides suggestions, but human judgement matters
