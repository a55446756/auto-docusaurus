# Talent Wall

The Talent Wall consolidates your talent in a single repository, supporting your resource management.

## Features

- **Automated profile creation**: Profiles created from CV uploads, including work experience, qualifications, skills, and security clearance
- **Manage talent**: Single location for candidate information, notes, contact tracking, and opportunity alignment

## How Tos

### How to create talent profiles {#create-profiles}

1. Click **Add CV** at the top right of the screen
2. Upload the candidate's CV (PDF or DOCX)
3. A row will appear with a pending indicator while FiOS processes the CV
4. Once processing completes, review and modify the profile as needed

### How to update notes against talent profiles

1. Click on the talent's row to open the candidate details panel
2. Adjust information in the textboxes
3. Click **Save** to save your updates

:::tip
Click on Security Clearance at the top of the panel to jump directly to that section.
:::

### How to add skills and competencies

1. Toggle the Key Skills section to **Detailed** view
2. Select **+ Add New Skill** at the bottom
3. Enter the skill/competency
4. Click the tick mark to confirm
5. Save the panel

### How to manage the relationship with talent

Key columns for relationship management:

**Status options:**
- **To be Contacted**: Candidates added but not yet reached out to
- **Waiting for Response**: Contacted, awaiting their response
- **Active Candidate**: Good candidate, not priority
- **Priority Candidate**: Must-have candidate
- **Rejected**: Non-suitable (won't be matched to opportunities)
- **Archived**: No longer needed on talent wall

**Last Contact**: Track when you last contacted each candidate

### How to manage opportunities associated with talent

The Opportunities tab shows three sections:

1. **Opportunity Management**: How many opportunities FiOS has assessed as suitable
2. **Opportunity Assessment**: Quick analysis of talent against current opportunities
3. **Assessment History**: Record of past opportunity matches

### How to manage talent documents

Upload and manage documents in the candidate's profile panel.

### Can I add 'remote' as a location?

Yes! Location is a free text field and accepts "Remote" as a valid location. FiOS recognises Remote as a location type.
